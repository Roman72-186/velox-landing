Service

AI integration for products that need practical automation, better retrieval, and smarter user flows.

We add AI where it creates actual leverage inside the product: support, search, summarization, classification, internal tooling, and workflow acceleration. The goal is usefulness, not novelty.

Where AI tends to help most

Customer-facing experiences
Assistants, guided search, knowledge retrieval, onboarding help, and message drafting.
Internal operations
Classification, summarization, routing, triage, enrichment, and repetitive workflow reduction.
Decision support
Document understanding, structured extraction, and product features driven by your own business data.

How we keep it usable

Good AI features need more than model calls. They need the right context, fallback behavior, evaluation logic, and a product flow that remains reliable when the model output is imperfect.

  • We define the exact task the AI feature should improve.
  • We connect the right data sources and guardrails.
  • We make the feature measurable and testable in context.

Typical engagement paths

Sometimes AI integration is a standalone feature project. Sometimes it is part of a broader platform build. We can work either way, as long as the product goal is clear.

Fast launch AI-first MVP Launch a focused product where the AI feature is central to the first user value proposition. Platform layer SaaS enhancement Embed assistants, retrieval, and automation into an existing SaaS platform without rewriting core flows.
